FORM AUTO-FILLER ON A MAC - FIRST TIME
======================================

You need: Google Chrome or Brave installed, and an internet connection.

1. Unzip the file you received (double-click it). You get a folder
   "Form-Auto-Filler". Move it to your Desktop or Documents.

2. Open the folder and double-click  setup.command

   The Mac will probably refuse the first time ("Apple could not verify..."
   or "cannot be opened"). That is normal for apps that are not from the
   App Store. Do this ONCE:
     - click "Done" (or "OK") in that message
     - open System Settings > Privacy & Security
     - scroll down: next to "setup.command was blocked" click "Open Anyway"
     - confirm with your Mac password, then click "Open"
   (On older Macs: right-click setup.command > Open > Open.)

3. A black window (Terminal) opens and sets everything up:
     - if Python is missing it downloads it and asks for YOUR MAC PASSWORD
       (the one that unlocks the computer). While you type it NOTHING
       appears on the screen - that is normal. Press Enter after it.
     - if the Mac asks "Terminal would like to access files in your
       Desktop/Documents/Downloads folder", click Allow.
   Wait until it says "Setup finished", then press Enter.

4. From now on, double-click  start.command  to open the app.
   Keep the black Terminal window open while you use the app (you can
   minimise it). Closing it closes the app.

The rest works like on Windows: see README.txt ("ON THE DAY" and "BEFORE IT
OPENS"). The first time, press CHECK FORM and sign in to Google in the browser
window that opens; the app remembers you after that.

To edit your answers ("Edit answers" opens TextEdit): keep the quotes " as
they are. If TextEdit turns them into curly quotes, the app fixes that itself.
